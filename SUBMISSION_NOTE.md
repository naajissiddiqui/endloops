# Endloops — Submission Note

### What I Decided to Build and Why
I designed and built a complete, production-grade website for Endloops using React, Vite, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS. The website centers on Endloops' core philosophy: *“The software prepares the work, and the professional reviews and decides.”* Rather than a generic software template, I focused on clearly explaining the distinction between **End-to-End Solutions** (turnkey suites for statutory audit, escrow audit, and budget preparation) and **Standalone Tools** (modular tools for purchase-order extraction, vouching, and expense schedules). The content is grounded in Endloops' real origins within Alkazim in Dubai, avoiding exaggerated claims or fabricated statistics.

### Most Important Design Decision
My most important design decision was rebuilding the hero visual as a realistic, component-driven product interface rather than an artificial screenshot. By presenting a clean commercial invoice alongside a purchase-order comparison that explicitly flags a line-item variance (+AED 250.00 freight discrepancy) under a “Review Pending” state, the first screen immediately communicates how Endloops operates: automated extraction with final sign-off preserved for the practitioner. Paired with a restrained editorial aesthetic, generous whitespace, and Manrope typography, the layout projects enterprise credibility.

### What I Would Improve with More Time
With more time, I would build an interactive file-upload sandbox allowing prospective clients to test sample PDF vouchers with client-side OCR and automated variance matching in real time, alongside streamlined keyboard shortcuts for rapid auditor review workflows.

### Tools Used & Time Spent
- **Stack**: React 18, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, Lucide React.
- **AI Tools**: Used for initial design system drafting, component scaffolding, and verifying copy against assignment requirements.
- **Time Spent**: Approximately 5.5 hours across design, development, visual QA, and testing.
